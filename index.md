# Teamvane

Simple, private apps for teams working in Atlassian products. Built on Atlassian Forge and running entirely on Atlassian's platform.

## Apps

**[Teamvane Planning Poker for Jira](/planning-poker/)** – private, real-time planning poker inside Jira. Votes stay hidden until the reveal, refinement sessions run through a queue of issues, and the agreed estimate saves to the issue in one click.
