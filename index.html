Simple, private apps for teams in Jira

Teamvane Planning Poker for Jira

Teamvane Retrospectives for Jira

How we build

Runs on Atlassian

No servers of our own. Our apps run on Atlassian Forge, and your data stays in your Atlassian site, following its data residency.

Private by design

We keep as little data as possible and delete it automatically. We never sell or share it, and there's no tracking or analytics.

Real support

Write to support@teamvane.com in English, French or Spanish, and get a reply within one business day.
